Information we collect

  • Contact details you share when you message us on WhatsApp or fill our enquiry form, such as your name, phone number and the details of your print job.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties except delivery partners strictly to fulfil your order, or where required by law.

Cookies

This site uses only minimal cookies necessary for core functionality. We do not use them to track you across other websites. You can clear or block cookies in your browser at any time.

Data retention

We keep your order and contact information only for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our legal and record-keeping obligations, after which it is securely deleted.

Your rights under the NDPA

You have the right to access the personal data we hold about you, to request correction or deletion, to withdraw consent, and to object to certain uses. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
