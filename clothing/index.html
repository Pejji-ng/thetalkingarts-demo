Before you order

Quick answers

How do I order?

Add what you want to your bag and tap Send order on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm stock and your total, then arrange payment and delivery with you. You can also just message us with what you need.

Can I really put my own words on a tee?

Yes. Type your line on the desk at the top of the page, choose a voice and a volume, and send it. We print it on our own press. If you have artwork instead of words, send that on WhatsApp.

What do the volume levels mean?

They set how big the print is. Whisper is a small print on the left chest. Talk sits in the middle of the chest. Loud fills the chest. Shout runs edge to edge.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. Collect from 23 Idimu Road, Ikotun (Monday to Saturday, 8AM to 7PM) or we deliver nationwide.

Can you print for a group?

Yes. Tell us the line, the number of people and when you need it, and we send one quote for the full set.
