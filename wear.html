Before you order

Quick answers

How do I order?

Add what you want to your bag and tap Send order on WhatsApp. We reply to confirm stock and your total, then arrange payment and delivery with you. You can also just message us with what you need.

Can you print my name and number?

Yes. Printing is what we do every day, so names and numbers are pressed in our own workshop. Try it in the Name & Number section and send us the result.

Do you have kids’ sizes?

Yes. We do kids’ sets with a shirt and shorts, printed with a name and number like the adult shirts. Tell us your child’s age and we will confirm the size.

Do you deliver outside Lagos?

Yes. Collect from 23 Idimu Road, Ikotun (Monday to Saturday, 8AM to 7PM) or we deliver nationwide.

My club is not on the rack. Can I still get it?

Message us the club and the kit you want. We will tell you what is available and how soon.

Do you make kits for teams and groups?

Yes. Mix your colours in the Team Kits section and send the design, or just tell us the colours, the number of players and any logos. We send one quote for the full set.
